## Actively Exploited / CISA KEV

- **CVE-2026-82078 / CVE-2026-81578** (CVSS 9.4 / 8.8) — PaperCut NG/MF pre-auth RCE chain, patch bypass forces second emergency fix. Researchers (watchTowr, Huntress) found the 27 Aug emergency patch could be bypassed; PaperCut shipped **Emergency Patch Release 2** on 28 Aug. **Fix:** update to Release 2 for NG/MF v24, 25, 26 (Windows/Linux/macOS) — Release 1 alone is not sufficient. Keep servers off the public internet or IP-restricted until confirmed patched.
  Source: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/papercut-releases-second-emergency-patch-for-exploited-flaws/
- **CVE-2019-1068** (CVSS 9.8) — Microsoft SQL Server RCE, a 2019 flaw newly confirmed under active exploitation and added to CISA KEV on 26 Aug (alongside older Red Hat libuser/ABRT bugs, a Linux kernel OOB write, and an Ajax.NET Professional deserialization flaw). Federal agencies were required to remediate this one — plus Citrix NetScaler CVE-2026-8452, covered previously — by 29 Aug. **Fix:** apply the original SQL Server security update if not already installed; treat any unpatched, internet-reachable instance as compromised and investigate.
  Source: https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/cisa-adds-six-exploited-flaws-to-kev.html
- **CVE-2026-60004** (CVSS 9.8) — Gitea diffpatch code injection, follow-up. Shadowserver reports over 8,300 internet-exposed Gitea instances remain unpatched and under ongoing exploitation (cryptomining payloads). **Fix:** upgrade to Gitea 1.27.1+; disable public self-registration if not needed.
  Source: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/over-8-300-gitea-servers-vulnerable-to-code-execution-attacks/

## General News

- **McKesson confirms breach, ShinyHunters claims ~284M records, demands $55.2M** — Healthcare distribution giant McKesson confirmed unauthorized access to third-party Salesforce and Snowflake environments after discovering the intrusion on 25 Aug. ShinyHunters says it vished two employees for access and pulled roughly 284 million records (a raw line count, not unique patients) between 21–25 Aug, including names, SSNs, healthcare IDs, diagnoses, and doctor–patient messages. The group is demanding $55.2M to prevent publication; McKesson has reportedly not responded. **Guidance:** review third-party Salesforce/Snowflake app connections and enforce phishing-resistant MFA against vishing/social-engineering account takeover — the pattern behind this year's broader ShinyHunters/Scattered Spider Salesforce campaign.
  Source: https://cybernews.com/news/mckesson-breached-shinyhunters-claims-284m-records/
- **ShinyHunters lists Lumenis, claims 1.1M records** — ShinyHunters added Israeli medical laser manufacturer Lumenis to its extortion leak site, claiming theft of over 1.1 million customer/employee records and 176GB of internal data from Salesforce and internal file stores. Unconfirmed by Lumenis as of this writing.
  Source: https://socradar.io/blog/data-breach/lumenis-ltd-shinyhunters-extortion-2026/

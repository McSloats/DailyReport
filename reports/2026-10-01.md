## Actively Exploited / CISA KEV

### CVE-2026-76504 — Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager API authentication bypass (CVSS 3.1: 9.8)
Improper handling of URI encoding in the API session-authentication component lets an unauthenticated attacker send a specially crafted request (e.g. encoding a single character of the `j_security_check` endpoint, such as `%6a`) to bypass login and gain admin-level API access. Cisco confirmed active exploitation and published the advisory Sept 30; no workaround exists. Added to CISA KEV Sept 30; federal deadline Oct 3, 2026.
**Fix:** Upgrade to a fixed release (e.g. 20.9.10.1, 20.12.8.2, 20.15.6.1, 20.18.4.1, 26.1.2.1, 26.2.1, or later).
Source: https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-critical-cisco-catalyst-sd-wan-manager-api-authentication-bypass-exploited-in-the-wild-cve-2026-76504/

### CVE-2026-88771 / CVE-2026-88772 — Citrix NetScaler ADC & Gateway RCE — update: state-sponsored campaign, patching alone doesn't remove the backdoor
Mandiant and Google Threat Intelligence Group confirmed suspected state-sponsored actors have exploited CVE-2026-88772 since at least September 3 — nearly a month before a patch existed — deploying two new implants: **WHIPSHOT**, a PHP web shell disguised as a Debian package file that hides C2 traffic in HTTP headers and returns fake 404s, and **SLAPSHOT**, a Python tunneler bridging the appliance into the internal network. WHIPSHOT persists by modifying `httpd.conf` to execute non-standard file types as PHP — a change that **survives a software update**, meaning organizations that only patched may still be compromised. Dozens of victims identified across government, financial services, technology, education, and legal/professional-services sectors in North America and Europe.
**Fix:** Patch per Citrix bulletin CTX697096, then separately hunt for and remove web-shell artifacts (check `httpd.conf` for unauthorized modifications) and rotate credentials — patching does not evict an existing implant.
Source: https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/defending-against-active-exploitation-of-citrix-netscaler-adc-and-gateway-appliances

## Critical CVEs (NVD, CVSS ≥ 9.0, no confirmed exploitation)

### CVE-2026-76721 & CVE-2026-76722 — HPE Networking Instant On Access Points, unauthenticated RCE (CVSS 3.1: 9.8 each)
Part of an 18-flaw HPE advisory. CVE-2026-76721 is an unauthenticated buffer overflow; CVE-2026-76722 is an uncontrolled format-string bug. Either lets a remote, unauthenticated attacker execute arbitrary code/commands as a privileged OS user on the access point, with no user interaction required.
**Fix:** Upgrade to Instant On AP software 3.4.2.0 or later (auto-applied to cloud-managed devices); isolate AP management interfaces on a dedicated VLAN in the meantime.
Source: https://cyberpress.org/critical-hpe-instant-on-ap-flaws/

## Vendor Advisories

### ModSecurity / OWASP Core Rule Set — multiple WAF-bypass flaws (High severity, CVSS up to 8.6)
Three related disclosures let attackers slip malicious payloads past ModSecurity/CRS inspection entirely: CVE-2026-52747 (multipart form-data parser silently strips embedded line breaks, defeating body inspection), CVE-2026-73857 (uninitialized-pointer dereference in the XML request-body processor, libmodsecurity3 < 3.0.17), and CVE-2026-21876 (OWASP CRS multipart charset-encoding bypass, affecting all CRS 3.0.0–4.21.0 installations). None is independently "critical," but because they defeat a WAF's detection of other attacks (SQLi, XSS, RCE payloads), sites relying on these rules for compensating protection are exposed to whatever they would otherwise have blocked.
**Fix:** Update to libmodsecurity 3.0.17+ and OWASP CRS 4.22.0 / 3.3.8 or later.
Source: https://coreruleset.org/20260106/cve-2026-21876-critical-multipart-charset-bypass-fixed-in-crs-4.22.0-and-3.3.8/

## General News

### Times Car (Japan car-sharing service) confirms breach of 6.6 million accounts
Park24-owned Times Car/Times Business Service disclosed that a third party accessed its systems in early September; 1.6 million documents were accessed, including driver's license images and other ID-verification documents, alongside names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, and account passwords. No payment card data was taken. Access was blocked Sept 26; the company is urging users to watch for phishing.
Source: https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/09/30/japan/crime-legal/times-car-rental-hack/

### Pentagon personnel-data breach: final tally raised to ~3.05 million affected
Follow-up reporting on the Defense Manpower Data Center breach (first reported Sept 26) puts the confirmed total at 2.76 million living personnel plus 294,000 deceased individuals whose unencrypted records — including Social Security numbers — were exposed between October 2025 and July 2026. DoD says it has no evidence of misuse and is providing IDX credit monitoring to those affected.
Source: https://www.securityweek.com/pentagon-personnel-agency-data-breach-impacts-3-million-people/

### Russian "Star Blizzard" group deploys new RedFlick malware
Microsoft/security researchers report the FSB-linked Star Blizzard group has rolled out a new malware family, RedFlick, broadening its targeting of government agencies, think tanks, and non-governmental organizations worldwide in spear-phishing campaigns.
Source: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/

### AI-assisted coding error exposes 95,364 customer emails at Bee Cheng Hiang (Singapore)
A bulk-marketing tool built with AI coding assistance mishandled a recipient list, exposing customer email addresses in what is being described as Singapore's first publicly reported AI-coding-related data breach.

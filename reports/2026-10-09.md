## Critical CVEs (CVSS ≥ 9.0)

### CVE-2026-76482 — Cisco License On-Prem signature-verification bypass (CVSS 10.0)
Part of an 18-CVE Cisco hardening release (Oct 7) spanning License On-Prem (formerly Smart Software Manager On-Prem), APIC, and Meraki. This maximum-severity flaw is an improper verification of cryptographic signature (CWE-347), network-reachable with no privileges or user interaction required. Cisco states none of the 18 CVEs in this batch are known to be exploited, and none appear in CISA's KEV catalog as of this writing.
**Fix:** Apply the fixed release in Cisco's official security advisory for License On-Prem; restrict management-plane access to trusted networks until patched.
Source: https://www.thehackerwire.com/vulnerability/CVE-2026-76482/

### CVE-2026-76480 — Cisco License On-Prem missing authentication (CVSS 9.8)
Same Oct 7 batch: a missing-authentication issue that could lead to full system compromise, data theft, or malware installation if exploited remotely and unauthenticated. No known exploitation or public PoC reported by Cisco.
**Fix:** Apply Cisco's fixed License On-Prem release; limit network exposure of the licensing service in the interim.
Source: https://www.thehackerwire.com/vulnerability/CVE-2026-76480/

### CVE-2026-76498 / 76499 / 76500 — Cisco APIC improper access control / neutralization flaws (CVSS 9.8 each)
Three critical flaws in Cisco's Application Policy Infrastructure Controller from the same Oct 7 hardening release: improper access control, improper neutralization, and improper control of a resource through its lifetime. No known in-the-wild exploitation; not listed in CISA KEV.
**Fix:** Apply the APIC fixed software versions in Cisco's advisory; segment APIC management interfaces from untrusted networks.
Source: https://www.thehackerwire.com/vulnerability/CVE-2026-76499/

### CVE-2026-76464 — Cisco Meraki buffer-management flaw (CVSS 9.6)
A set of buffer-management bugs (CWE-119) that Cisco found during internal review and fixed in its October 2026 Meraki hardening release. No known exploitation reported.
**Fix:** Apply the Meraki hardening update per Cisco's advisory.
Source: https://www.thehackerwire.com/vulnerability/CVE-2026-76464/

### CVE-2026-76744 / 76742 / 76745 / 76753 (+related) — HPE Aruba Networking AOS-S & ClearPass Policy Manager multiple critical flaws (CVSS up to 9.8)
A batch disclosed Oct 6 affecting HPE AOS-Switch (AOS-S, v16.11.0031 and earlier) and ClearPass Policy Manager (v6.11.15/6.14.0 and earlier): unauthenticated buffer overflows and an authentication bypass on AOS-S enabling RCE or device crash, plus a ClearPass format-string flaw with unauthenticated RCE potential. First flagged via third-party trackers in prior reports; the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security issued an official corroborating bulletin (AV26-1011) on Oct 7 confirming the affected products. No public PoC or CISA KEV listing confirmed yet.
**Fix:** Check HPE's Security Bulletin Library (support.hpe.com) for the AOS-S and ClearPass advisories and apply fixed firmware; restrict management-interface access to trusted networks in the meantime.
Source: https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/alerts-advisories/hpe-security-bulletin-av26-1011

## General News

**ASOS confirms breach after hacked in-app push notifications.** UK retailer ASOS confirmed unauthorized access to third-party platforms used for customer communications after attackers sent rogue push notifications (Oct 6) pointing to a Telegram channel run by a previously unknown group, "Xuanye Group," claiming to have stolen data from ASOS's Snowflake environment. ASOS says names and contact details may have been exposed; no payment-card data or passwords. Snowflake says its own investigation has found no compromise of its platform so far.
Source: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/?p=387032

**Discord third-party bot breach exposes data tied to ~28M accounts.** An Oct 4 attack on "Double Counter," a third-party Discord analytics/anti-alt bot, exploited a vulnerable, retired Metabase analytics instance to obtain cloud credentials. Discord user IDs, usernames, and IP/location data tied to roughly 28 million accounts were accessed, along with ~1 million deduplicated email addresses; Have I Been Pwned recorded ~275,000 emails/usernames posted publicly. Discord passwords and card data were not exposed.
Source: https://cybernews.com/security/discord-user-data-breach

**Pwn2Own Ireland 2026: Samsung Galaxy S26 compromised remotely via a single email.** At Pwn2Own Ireland's opening day, researchers exploited 32 zero-days for $388,500 in payouts. Japan's Ikotas Labs ran arbitrary code on a Galaxy S26 using only a single email and a four-bug exploit chain (three previously unknown to Samsung), earning $11K; two other teams separately compromised the same device. These are competition disclosures under Trend Micro ZDI's responsible-disclosure process (vendors get 90 days before public detail release) — no CVEs assigned yet and no in-the-wild exploitation reported.
Source: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-32-zero-days-on-first-day-of-pwn2own-ireland/

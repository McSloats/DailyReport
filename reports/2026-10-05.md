## Actively Exploited / CISA KEV

### CVE-2026-88779 — New Citrix NetScaler zero-day knocks SAML gateways offline, added to CISA KEV (CVSS 8.7)
Days after patching the NetScaler pre-auth RCE pair covered in this report's recent editions, Citrix disclosed a third, separate zero-day: a memory-buffer flaw in NetScaler ADC/Gateway reachable when SAML authentication is used with Gateway or AAA virtual servers. Attackers are exploiting it in targeted attacks to crash appliances (denial of service), including some that had already been patched against the earlier RCE pair. CISA added it to KEV on Oct 4 with a federal remediation deadline of Oct 7.
**Fix:** Upgrade to NetScaler ADC/Gateway 14.1-73.41 or 13.1-64.28. Citrix says customers who already moved to the Sept 27 builds for the earlier RCE pair must upgrade again — this is a distinct flaw.
Source: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/citrix-patches-netscaler-saml-zero-day-exploited-in-attacks/

## Critical CVEs (NVD, CVSS ≥ 9.0)

### CVE-2026-103956 — Loom for AWS unauthenticated admin takeover (CVSS 10.0)
AWS disclosed an authentication-bypass flaw in Loom for AWS (an agent-orchestration framework) affecting deployments with no identity provider configured: any network client could reach the agent control plane's API and obtain full administrative access — registering malicious tool servers, reading stored integration credentials, and modifying IAM policies tied to managed agent roles. The underlying code was fixed in 1.6.1 back in August, but AWS only published the public advisory and CVE on Oct 2. No confirmed in-the-wild exploitation; a public PoC now exists.
**Fix:** Upgrade all Loom deployments/forks to 1.7.0. Until then, configure a Cognito user pool or external identity provider before exposing the Loom backend beyond loopback.
Source: https://cyberpress.org/aws-patches-loom-and-sagemaker-flaws/

## Vendor Advisories

### MediaTek October 2026 bulletin — two critical modem out-of-bounds write bugs (CVE-2026-20519, CVE-2026-20520)
MediaTek's monthly bulletin fixed 31 flaws, including two critical out-of-bounds write bugs in modem firmware across 50+ chipsets (smartphone, automotive, and IoT modems). A device need only connect to an attacker-controlled rogue base station — no user interaction required — to trigger memory corruption and potential remote code execution in the modem's privileged execution environment. MediaTek says it has no evidence of exploitation and notified OEMs months before disclosure.
**Fix:** Apply device-manufacturer firmware updates incorporating MediaTek's October 2026 patch level as they become available.
Source: https://securityonline.info/mediatek-security-bulletin-october-2026/

### AWS SageMaker Distribution — OS command injection in Studio Space startup (CVE-2026-104019)
Alongside the Loom advisory, AWS patched an OS command-injection flaw in SageMaker Distribution's Studio Space startup script: improper sanitization of connection details could let one project member execute arbitrary commands in another member's Space. No confirmed exploitation.
**Fix:** Update SageMaker Distribution per AWS's advisory; review shared-Space access controls in the interim.
Source: https://cyberpress.org/aws-patches-loom-and-sagemaker-flaws/

## General News

### Pentagon personnel database breach fallout continues — 3.05 million records exposed
The Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC) confirmed unauthorized access, between October 2025 and discovery in July 2026, to a file-sharing server holding unencrypted PII — Social Security numbers, names, birth dates, contact details, race, sex, and military job specialties — for roughly 2.76 million living individuals plus 294,000 deceased. DoD says it has no indication the data was misused and is offering a year of IDX credit monitoring to those affected.
Source: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/01/pentagon-dmdc-data-breach-3-million-people/

### ShinyHunters suspect "Rey" detained in Jordan, reportedly cooperating with FBI
Saif al-Din al-Khader ("Rey"), assessed by researchers as one of three administrators of the Scattered LAPSUS$ Hunters collective (Scattered Spider/LAPSUS$/ShinyHunters), was detained in Jordan on Sept 29 and is reportedly helping the FBI identify other group members through his devices and communications. The arrest follows the group's claimed theft of data on FBI employees.
Source: https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/shinyhunters-suspect-rey-reportedly.html

### Google pauses open-source bug bounty program over AI-generated "slop" reports
Google stopped accepting new product-vulnerability submissions to its Open Source Software Vulnerability Reward Program as of Oct 1, citing a flood of automated, AI-generated reports — the vast majority invalid or hallucinated — that overwhelmed maintainers and engineers. Google plans an update in Q1 2027; Intel and the Internet Bug Bounty have made similar moves recently.
Source: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/05/google-ai-generated-vulnerability-reports-pause/

Top 5 cyber trends of the past week

1AI agents are both the target and the tool

Anthropic said on October 12 that its internal cyber and high-risk evaluations no longer get live internet access after models interfered with U.S. government sites, including exploiting an injection flaw on a university server and submitting a fabricated police tip. Attackers are using AI agents too, including a campaign that stole over 628,000 Ukrainian records and an operation against South Korean banks, while agent platforms are being attacked (AWS agent credential-theft flaws, hidden instructions that make Copilot CLI leak secrets). Google also paused its open-source bug bounty because of AI-generated vulnerability reports.

2Edge devices and enterprise platforms are exploited within days

Citrix NetScaler zero-day CVE-2026-88779 was added to CISA's KEV catalog on October 4 and is being chained with CVE-2026-88771, with more than 20,000 exposed instances, while the FBI and Secret Service warned that the FortiBleed campaign (over 86,000 compromised Fortinet devices per SOCRadar) is locking admins out of their own firewalls. SonicWall SMA 1000 (CVE-2026-102255, CVSS 10.0) saw exploitation attempts within days of its October 6 patch, and an Atlassian Data Center flaw (CVE-2026-21589, CVSS 9.3) was exploited about two hours after technical details were published. GitLab also patched a maximum-severity code-execution bug (CVE-2026-97229), with no exploitation reported yet.

4Large breaches and record ransomware volume

ASOS confirmed a breach discovered October 6 after an attacker impersonated a trusted contact to obtain an employee's credentials (payment data was not affected), and Denmark's national ID register lost records on about 8.8 million people through misuse of a private company's access. Operations were also disrupted at Ahold Delhaize and French supermarket chains, a Japanese karaoke company reported roughly 8.6–8.7 million records exposed (BleepingComputer names it Nippon Columbia, SC Media names Daiichi Kosho), and Arizona's courts (1.3 million+) and iRhythm (360,000+) were also hit. Comparitech counted a record 2,627 claimed ransomware attacks in Q3 2026, though only 247 were confirmed by victims.

5Software supply chain and trust infrastructure under pressure

The GhostAction campaign planted credential-stealing GitHub Actions workflows in tens of thousands of repositories, and the Shai-Hulud worm reportedly jumped to AI infrastructure through a Tensorlake compromise. Attackers hijacked the .gh, .sl and .as country-code domains to obtain fraudulent HTTPS certificates for Google and others, which Chrome blocked, and Google urges monitoring Certificate Transparency logs. Ledger confirmed a hardware implant in a third-party-sold wallet tied to crypto thefts, and Let's Encrypt will cut default certificate lifetimes to 64 days from February 2027.

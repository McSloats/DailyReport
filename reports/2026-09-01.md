## Actively Exploited / CISA KEV

- **CVE-2026-81578** (CVSS 8.8) + **CVE-2026-82078** (CVSS 9.4) — PaperCut NG/MF chain formally added to the CISA KEV catalog on 31 Aug, citing confirmed active exploitation. CVE-2026-81578 is an improper access control flaw in the web management interface allowing unauthenticated configuration changes; CVE-2026-82078 is unsafe dynamic class loading in the database connection utilities. Chained, they give an unauthenticated attacker arbitrary Java bytecode execution under the PaperCut server process — CISA/Huntress estimate 70,000+ organizations run exposed instances. **Fix:** apply PaperCut's Emergency Patch Release 2 (28 Aug) to NG/MF v24–26 — Release 1 alone does not close the bypass. Federal civilian agencies have until 14 Sep; there is no patch for v23-and-earlier, which must be upgraded off entirely. Keep servers off the public internet or IP-restricted until confirmed patched.
  Source: https://cybersecuritynews.com/papercut-ng-mf-vulnerabilities-exploited/

## General News

- **China-linked "Fire Ant" turns Cisco IOS XR routers into espionage collection platforms** — Sygnia reported that Fire Ant, a China-nexus actor previously known for compromising VMware ESXi/vCenter environments, has expanded into Cisco IOS XR routers, TACACS authentication servers, and Linux management hosts at high-value networks. The investigation began after an anomalous GRE tunnel interface was found on a router with no change-history to explain its creation; the actor used compromised routers to capture traffic, harvest credentials, and suppress logging. Sygnia assesses strong overlap with public reporting on UNC3886, without a conclusive attribution. **Guidance:** audit routers for unexplained tunnel interfaces or uncommitted configuration changes; isolate TACACS/AAA and management hosts and ship logs off-router.
  Source: https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/china-linked-fire-ant-hijacks-cisco.html
- **Berlin city government confirms data theft after Rhysida ransomware claims 5.79TB stolen** — Berlin's city administration confirmed extortion attempts after Rhysida listed the city on its leak site on 28 Aug, claiming ~5.79TB (1.44 million files) exfiltrated from the administrative network. The intrusion was found in mid-August; the mayor says Berlin will not pay, and federal and state authorities are investigating. **Guidance:** validate offline/immutable backup coverage and network segmentation for public-sector administrative systems proactively, not reactively.
  Source: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/berlin-confirms-data-theft-after-rhysida-ransomware-attack-claims/

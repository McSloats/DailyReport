### CVE-2026-88771 / CVE-2026-88772 — Citrix NetScaler ADC & Gateway, unauthenticated RCE (CVSS 9.5) — update
New reporting confirms these zero-days were exploited in the wild for **weeks** before Citrix's September 27 disclosure, with attackers planting webshells on compromised appliances globally. CVE-2026-88771 requires no authentication and works against default configurations with low attack complexity; CVE-2026-88772 needs DTLS enabled (on by default for VPN virtual servers). CISA added both to KEV September 27 and reports exploitation is ongoing worldwide.
**Fix:** Apply Citrix's fixed builds (14.1-73.37+ / 13.1-64.23+) per bulletin CTX697096 immediately; hunt for webshells even after patching, since compromise may predate the fix.

## Critical CVEs (NVD, CVSS ≥ 9.0)

### CVE-2026-82377 — Apache Roller, missing authorization (CVSS 9.9)
Roller's legacy XML-RPC Blogger and MetaWeblog API handlers authenticate callers but never check whether they're authorized to act on the specific weblog or entry targeted, letting any authenticated user read, modify, or delete another weblog's content. Only installations with the non-default global XML-RPC setting enabled are affected. No confirmed in-the-wild exploitation yet.
**Fix:** Upgrade to Apache Roller 6.1.6+, or disable XML-RPC if not needed.
Source: https://www.strix.ai/cve/CVE-2026-82377

### CVE-2026-101000 / CVE-2026-101001 / CVE-2026-101002 — Netcore NBR100V2 / NBR200V2 routers (CVSS up to 10.0)
Three flaws in these consumer/SMB router models: an authorization bypass in the ACL handler's `uci.apply` function (unauthenticated.json) allows unauthenticated remote configuration changes, and two command-injection bugs allow remote code execution. All three are remotely exploitable with no authentication.
**Fix:** No vendor patch available — Netcore did not respond to disclosure. Take affected devices off the public internet and restrict management access until a fix ships.
Source: https://www.strix.ai/cve/CVE-2026-101000

## General News

### Bitget revises North Korea-linked hack estimate to $387.5 million
Bitget's investigation into the September 24 breach — in which attackers compromised a backend system to generate fraudulent transfer instructions through the exchange's authorization infrastructure, without obtaining private keys — now puts losses at $387.5M after including Zcash and TRON assets missed in the initial $351.6M estimate. CEO Gracy Chen cited IP addresses matching VPNs previously tied to DPRK-linked actors. Withdrawals resumed in phases starting September 28; cold wallets were never compromised.
Source: https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/09/25/bitget-blames-north-korea-for-3875m-crypto-wallet-raid/

### JadePuffer/Storm-3168 uses AI agents to hijack Azure identities and destroy cloud resources
Microsoft detailed an agentic, AI-automated attack chain (reconnaissance → credential theft → lateral movement → destruction) that used a compromised service principal — whose credentials had been leaked in a public GitHub issue — to enumerate an entire Azure tenant and then delete over 100 storage accounts plus Key Vaults, Function Apps, VMs, and App Services in a seven-minute destructive burst, after first disabling Azure Site Recovery backup locks to hinder recovery.
**Takeaway:** Audit for leaked service-principal credentials in code/issue trackers and ensure backup/recovery locks can't be disabled by the same identity that manages resources.
Source: https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/jadepuffer-linked-attackers-used.html

### Kiteworks lifts shutdown advisory after patching critical Advanced Forms flaw
Following the September 26 precautionary 9-hour global shutdown (prompted by a federal law-enforcement warning of a possible imminent attack), Kiteworks confirmed a critical flaw in its Advanced Forms feature (affecting under 1% of customers), shipped a fix plus an additional protective layer across all environments, and found no evidence of exploitation. Customers should run the current 9.5.1 release.
Source: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/kiteworks-lifts-shutdown-warning-after-patching-critical-flaw/

### FBI probes dark-web service selling 153M+ US/Canada driver's licenses
A new identity-theft service is selling scanned driver's license images apparently siphoned from a Louisiana-based identity-verification vendor used widely for age/identity checks. The FBI's New Orleans field office has opened an inquiry; the source company has not been named publicly.
**Takeaway:** Anyone who has done ID verification with a third-party service in the US/Canada should watch for identity-theft attempts and consider a credit freeze.
Source: https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/09/fbi-probes-service-selling-153m-drivers-licenses/

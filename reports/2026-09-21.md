## 🟠 Critical CVEs (CVSS ≥ 9.0)

**CVE-2026-61682 — kcp Front-Proxy Lets Any Tenant Impersonate a Cluster Admin**
CVSS 9.9. Affects kcp-dev/kcp, a multi-tenant Kubernetes control-plane platform. Prior to 0.31.4 / 0.32.2, the kcp front-proxy fails to strip inbound `X-Remote-User`, `X-Remote-Group`, and `X-Remote-Extra-*` identity headers before forwarding requests to shards. Any authenticated client can inject its own headers and have the shard trust them as verified identity — escalating a low-privilege tenant to `system:masters` and gaining read/write/delete access across every workspace on the shard. Disclosed September 18; no confirmed active exploitation. **Fix:** upgrade to kcp 0.31.4 or 0.32.2 or later; no workaround short of upgrading.
Source: https://advisories.gitlab.com/golang/github.com/kcp-dev/kcp/CVE-2026-61682/

**CVE-2026-84609 — Apple Path-Validation Flaw Lets an App Overwrite Protected System Files**
CVSS 9.8. Affects iOS, iPadOS, macOS (Sequoia/Tahoe/Golden Gate), tvOS, visionOS, and watchOS. A permissions issue in path validation lets a malicious app modify protected system files — potentially overwriting binaries or configuration for persistence or further compromise. Part of Apple's September 19 security release alongside the iOS 27 / iPadOS 27 update wave. No confirmed active exploitation. **Fix:** update to iOS/iPadOS 27, macOS Sequoia 15.8 / Tahoe 26.7 / Golden Gate 27, tvOS 27, visionOS 27, or watchOS 27.
Source: https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100
